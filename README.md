## Project Overview

This project analyses the sales and stock performance of Wanjiku's fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market. Wanjiku needs to understand her daily and weekly sales patterns, identify strong and weak sales days, compare weekday and weekend performance, and monitor stock levels to make better business decisions. Using core Python, this analysis calculates sales statistics, classifies daily performance, identifies products requiring restocking, and calculates gross profit margins to provide practical recommendations for improving business operations.

## Top Three Findings

1. **Total Sales:** The stall generated total sales of **KES 45,450** over the 14-day analysis period, with average daily sales of **KES 3,246.43**.

2. **Weekend Performance:** Average weekend sales were **KES 4,900**, compared to weekday average sales of **KES 2,885**. Weekend sales were approximately **69.84% higher**.

3. **Stock Management:** Tomatoes and Sukuma required restocking. Tomatoes had 18 units against a reorder level of 25, while Sukuma had 9 units against a reorder level of 15.
